Our approach

Viregro is built on a simple idea: healthy soil and water systems already contain living biology that does the real work. Our products are designed to support that biology rather than overpower it with harsh chemicals.

  • Natural & plant-derived. Made from natural ingredients — no harsh synthetic chemicals.
  • Works with living systems. Designed to support the microorganisms already present in soil, septic systems, and drain lines.
  • Made in Florida. Formulated and produced in the U.S.
